== What I built

A single-node Prometheus stack under Podman, chosen so that every exam domain
has something running behind it rather than only a paragraph in my notes.

[cols="1,1,3"]
|===
| Service | Port | Exam domain it serves

| prometheus     | 9090 | Fundamentals, PromQL
| alertmanager   | 9093 | Alerting: routing, grouping, inhibition, silences
| grafana        | 3000 | Dashboarding
| node-exporter  | 9100 | The USE method, `node_cpu_seconds_total`
| blackbox       | 9115 | Black-box probing and the `__param_target` relabel dance
| pushgateway    | 9091 | Batch-job metrics and their caveats
| sample-app     | 8000 | One of each metric type: counter, gauge, histogram, summary
| loadgen        | -    | Traffic, so the counters and histograms hold real data
|===

The one that took me longest to internalise is the blackbox job, because
Prometheus scrapes the *exporter* and passes the real target as a URL parameter:

.`lab/prometheus/prometheus.yml`, the blackbox relabel chain
[source,yaml]
----
include::../../lab/prometheus/prometheus.yml[tag=blackbox]
----

TIP: Read the three `relabel_configs` steps in order. The target becomes the
query parameter, the parameter is preserved as the `instance` label, and only
then is `__address__` overwritten with the exporter's address. Reversing the
last two loses the real target's identity.

== What I got wrong

* Assumed `blackbox-icmp` was broken. It is not: rootless containers cannot send
  ICMP, so `probe_success == 0` there is correct behaviour. I left it in as a
  permanently failing target to alert against.
* Expected the provisioned Grafana dashboard on the home page. "Recent
  dashboards" stays empty until you open one. It lives under
  menu:Dashboards[PCA Lab].
